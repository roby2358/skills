Game where the core dynamic is being THE HEALER for hire across different groups of adventurers. Moment-to-moment: triage under mana scarcity — multiple wounded, not enough heals, choosing who dies. Key constraint that makes it a game: you do NOT control the party; you're a goalie reacting to allies who walk into their own doom (tank over-pulls, rogue stands in fire). Drivers it nails: Guardianship (you are the "nobody who heals"), Loss Aversion (prevent death, not collect loot), Scarcity of Agency (triage), Comedy (cleaning up after reckless adventurers). Revenge driver deliberately doesn't fit — healing resists it. Meta-layer = reputation/prestige: contracts and pay scale with your healer reputation, so it climbs the activity ladder the same way En Garde does (low verb = healing input, high verb = prestige scoreboard). Lands in the "healing as dominant mechanic" whitespace — barely any games exist here. Companion idea to the En Garde (GDW) prestige-game build.
